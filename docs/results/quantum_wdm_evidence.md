# Quantum-focused evidence from real optical conditions

## What was tested

The QC-PEB sources answer different parts of the thesis and are deliberately
not merged into one validation claim.

1. IrelandQCI/Toshiba supplies controlled classical optical injection and
   measured QKD outcomes (QBER and secure key rate), but no timestamps and no
   Bell fidelity.
2. Bristol supplies timestamped multichannel field WDM telemetry, but no
   quantum outcome. Its Bell fidelity is generated by the repository's
   original Qiskit/Aer channel model.
3. Bristol HCF supplies ordered On/Off coexistence conditions, measured QBER
   and SKR, and three contemporaneous unexposed links, but no WDM vector or
   absolute timestamps.
4. Dryad STIKE supplies a 168-hour measured-QBER and communication-rate series,
   but no WDM telemetry, SKR or fidelity. Its public file download was blocked
   during the reference phase, so only the adapter/protocol is present.
5. Gavignet et al. supplies a third real DWDM/QKD platform with published
   condition means for WDM power/channel count, classical BER/OSNR and measured
   QBER/SKR, but no public measurement-level files or independent-unit metadata.

The IrelandQCI and Bristol HCF arms test real physical coexistence effects.
The Bristol field arm tests whether recorded cross-channel WDM dynamics add
temporal information in the same simulation used by the original repository.
Dryad is a real-quantum own-history baseline, not a WDM test.

## Measured QKD result

The experimental unit is one mean per fibre/VOA/signal cell, not each repeated
reading. Sixteen matched fibre/VOA strata compare baseline against the average
of the four injected-signal conditions. Corrected intervals use 50,000
bootstrap replicates; p-values are exact one-sided sign-flip tests over all
`2^16` assignments, Bonferroni-corrected for two primary targets.

| Measured outcome | Mean degradation | Corrected 95% family interval | Corrected p | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|:---:|
| QBER, published scale | +0.013204 | [+0.001006, +0.046547] | 0.00150 | positive |
| log1p secure key rate | +0.360128 | [+0.051003, +0.793668] | 0.01147 | positive |

For QBER, positive means injection minus baseline; for key rate, positive
means baseline minus injection. The QBER scale is not interpreted as ratio or
percentage because the publisher documents it ambiguously as `ratio (or %)`.

The dose-family result was concentrated at stronger injection: QBER
degradation was corrected-significant at 12 dBm, while secure-key-rate
degradation was corrected-significant at 9 and 12 dBm. Lower-power contrasts
did not survive the eight-test family correction.

This is direct evidence of a matched dependence between a controlled classical
optical coexistence condition and measured performance of a real QKD system,
consistent with degradation from injection. The publisher does not document
randomized session order, so residual alignment/session drift prevents a
fully randomized causal interpretation. It is not a fidelity measurement and
it is not forecasting.

The leave-one-fibre/VOA-stratum-out predictive test was negative for both
targets. Adding coexistence conditions gave a point MAE improvement of
`+0.1063` for log key rate, but its corrected interval crossed zero
`[-0.0223, +0.2719]`; QBER prediction worsened by `-0.006675`
`[-0.01136, -0.00259]`. Controlled dependence and cross-stratum predictive
generalization are therefore reported as different results.

## Bristol HCF measured-QKD and robustness result

Using adjacent On/Off pairs and `BC/BD/CD` as simultaneous controls, the mean
degradation difference-in-differences was `0.015266` QBER proportion and
`0.106588` on `log1p(SKR)`. Both survived the predeclared two-target correction.
The QC-PEB sensitivity pass then kept both directions positive in every
contiguous segment and after removing any segment, link or pair. The documented
Alice group ranked first among all 20 spatial three-link assignments.

This strengthens spatial/localized coexistence evidence but does not change
the claim class. The robustness families were added after data inspection,
the exposure topology was not randomized, and the two-sided spatial tail
fraction is `0.10`. Perfect On/Off alternation makes lag-shift label placebos
non-independent: lag 1 reverses treatment and lag 2 reproduces it. See
[`bristol_hcf_coexistence.md`](bristol_hcf_coexistence.md) and
[`qc_peb_phase1.md`](qc_peb_phase1.md).

## Cross-platform directional replication

Combining only valid study-unit effects—not raw rows—the same positive
coexistence direction replicated in IrelandQCI and Bristol for QBER
(`Holm p=0.001495`) and `log1p(SKR)` (`Holm p=0.005737`). Effect magnitudes were
strongly heterogeneous (`I²=95.4%` and `79.9%`), and both two-study
random-effects intervals crossed zero. The result is therefore directional
replication, not a transferable common effect size. Full details are in
[`cross_platform_qkd_replication.md`](cross_platform_qkd_replication.md).

## Third-platform descriptive corroboration

Gavignet et al.'s 50 km single-span means change from `3.4%` QBER and
`169 kb/s` SKR without WDM to `5.4%` and `107/106 kb/s` with 30/60 channels.
The two exposed contrasts therefore agree with the replicated adverse
direction. In its multispan MU-QKD arm, increasing total WDM power from 13.5
to 17.5 dBm improves classical OSNR (`26.2` to `29.7 dB`) and pre-FEC BER
(`1e-2` to `3e-3`) while worsening QBER (`5.4%` to `6.7%`) and SKR (`63` to
`36.5 kb/s`).

This is scientifically useful because it reveals an antagonistic
classical/quantum tradeoff: “classical link quality” cannot be used as a
one-dimensional proxy for quantum health. The values are only published
condition aggregates, so they add external physical plausibility, not a third
formal replication, uncertainty estimate or temporal forecast. See
[`qc_peb_phase3.md`](qc_peb_phase3.md).

The subsequent joint-objective audit makes that implication executable. All
three Gavignet multispan conditions are non-dominated across BER, OSNR, QBER
and SKR. On IrelandQCI, a fixed Pareto-aware TriHead improves dominance-pair
accuracy over its permuted-condition control after multiplicity correction,
but complete Pareto-set recovery remains low and superiority over the
conventional TriHead is inconclusive. The result supports contemporaneous
condition-ordering information and a Pareto decision representation, but not
temporal predictive superiority; see
[`classical_quantum_pareto.md`](classical_quantum_pareto.md).

A post-discovery falsification audit strengthens and narrows that statement.
Dominance ordering for both frozen TriHeads exceeds a 20,000-draw joint-label
null after Bonferroni, but neither model beats a within-stratum target-mean
baseline when a complete WDM condition is held out. The signal is therefore
specific to ordering within the observed grid and cannot support unseen-power
or temporal-forecast claims; see
[`qkd_pareto_falsification.md`](qkd_pareto_falsification.md).

A subsequent monotonic-dose falsification narrows it again. A target-free rule
that ranks no injection, 3, 7, 9 and 12 dBm from least to most adverse reaches
`0.6784` dominance accuracy, significantly above the Pareto-aware TriHead's
`0.5625` after correction. The current network result therefore does not show
residual ordering structure beyond power dose; see
[`qkd_dose_order_falsification.md`](qkd_dose_order_falsification.md).

The raw-reading hierarchical audit strengthens the measurement side while
exposing another design boundary. QBER and `log1p(SKR)` degradation remain
positive under VOA-stratified resampling, within-cell blocks of 1/5/10 readings
and every deletion sensitivity. At block length 10, corrected conditional
intervals are `[0.000966, 0.036818]` and `[0.075857, 0.733341]`. However, every
distance/condition has only one sweep session, so condition and session effects
cannot be separated. See
[`qkd_hierarchical_robustness.md`](qkd_hierarchical_robustness.md).

## Bristol real-WDM/Aer replay result

Recorded BER and simulated fidelity had Pearson `r=-0.999875`, but this number
is not evidence: BER is an input to the imposed depolarization equation. The
non-circular estimand is

`MAE(own simulated-fidelity history) - MAE(own history + other recorded WDM channels)`.

It was evaluated at 15 and 60 minutes (one and four 15-minute steps) using two
purged expanding-origin folds, a 20-step window, 20,000 paired moving-block
bootstrap replicates and Bonferroni correction over 16 contrasts.

| Horizon | Model | Improvement over AR | Improvement over permuted WDM | Joint evidence |
|---:|---|---:|---:|:---:|
| 15 min | EdgeGRU | -0.005909 | -0.001025 | no |
| 15 min | EdgeLSTM | -0.013431 | -0.000466 | no |
| 15 min | EdgeTCN | +0.011223 | -0.001802 | no |
| 15 min | Ridge | -0.000212 | -0.000111 | no |
| 60 min | EdgeGRU | -0.005972 | -0.000997 | no |
| 60 min | EdgeLSTM | -0.017145 | -0.000223 | no |
| 60 min | EdgeTCN | +0.011197 | -0.001488 | no |
| 60 min | Ridge | -0.000329 | -0.000207 | no |

EdgeTCN beat the autoregressive feature set at both horizons with corrected
positive intervals, but aligned WDM was worse than the temporally permuted
control. Thus **0/8** model/horizon combinations met the joint evidence rule.
The TCN result can reflect added feature structure or regularization, but not
verified information in the temporal alignment of the other WDM channels.

## Thesis-safe conclusion

Two independent measured-QKD sources now formally directionally replicate a
narrower but important form of the central thesis: classical optical
coexistence conditions contain information about real QKD performance.
IrelandQCI shows a dose-concentrated
adverse association robust conditional on its published sweep sessions;
Bristol HCF shows localized
On/Off degradation that remains directionally stable across the audited
segments, links and switch pairs. A third DWDM/QKD platform agrees at the
published-aggregate level and exposes an opposing classical/quantum response
to launch power. The
current evidence does **not** establish learned ordering beyond the monotonic
power prior or that passive timestamped WDM telemetry predicts future
Bell-state fidelity. The original simulated correlation is
reproduced on real BER inputs, but its direct link remains model-imposed; the
stronger cross-channel temporal control was negative.

The decisive next dataset must record synchronized WDM telemetry and a
quantum target on the same link and clock—preferably QBER/SKR plus visibility,
tomography-derived entangled-state fidelity, or heralded-pair statistics—so a
future-target model can be compared against quantum-target autoregression
without an imposed BER-to-fidelity coefficient.
